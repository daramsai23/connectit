How it works
From signup to hired in 3 steps
STEP 01
Verify your identity
Students verify with a driver's license tied to their primary address. Vendors verify with their company EIN or tax ID. No fake accounts, no ghost candidates.
STEP 02
Match by location & skills
Recruiters search for students within a custom radius of each job posting — filter by visa, contract type, tech stack, designation, and work mode all at once.
STEP 03
Apply and get hired
Students apply directly from the job detail page. Recruiters review, shortlist, and reach out — the full pipeline from discovery to offer in one place.
