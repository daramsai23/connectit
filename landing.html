HOW TO JOIN
Two steps to get discovered
1
Create your profile

Build your profile with real contact details — students and professionals both register the same way. Everything is validated at entry.

  • Email address — personal or university email
  • Real US carrier phone only — no VoIP or Google Voice
  • Your city, tech stack, and years of experience
  • Work authorization status and contract preference
⚠ 
Not accepted: Google Voice, TextNow, TextFree, Burner, Hushed, or any VoIP number. Verified via carrier lookup API at signup.
2
Verify your identity

One-time identity verification — done in under 2 minutes. After this, your profile is trusted by every vendor on the platform.

  • Driver's License required — front and back upload
  • Location must match — DL address matches your registered city
  • Live selfie check — confirms you match the document
  • No duplicate accounts — one person, one verified profile
Powered by Persona — Verified once, trusted forever. You will never be asked to re-upload documents.

MODULE 05
Part Time
Project-based part-time work exclusively for Green Card holders and US Citizens. Strictly 1099 or W2 only. No sponsorship. No C2C.
Who can participate
Part-time project work is restricted to Green Card holders and US Citizens only — enforced at verification before access is granted.
GC & Citizen only
Allowed contract types
  • 1099 — Independent contractor
    Self-employed, handles own taxes
  • W2 — Through employer payroll
    Taxes withheld by employer
No C2C, no corp-to-corp, no sponsorship arrangements in this section.
Project-based only
  • Fixed scope and timeline
  • Defined deliverables
  • Hourly or milestone billing
  • Fully remote or hybrid
  • Max 20–30 hours per week
⚠ 
Strict enforcement: Any user who misrepresents their work authorization to access part-time roles will be permanently banned. Work auth is verified against the Driver's License submitted during identity verification.
